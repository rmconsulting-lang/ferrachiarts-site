Book of Enoch 40-day reading plan and study guide

Walking with Enoch takes you through all 108 chapters of 1 Enoch in forty short daily readings. The full text is included.

This is a companion for reading the Book of Enoch, not a substitute for the text. Each day tells you which chapters to read, then gives a plain-English summary, one key quotation from the public-domain R. H. Charles translation (1917), three questions, a short history note, and lined space to write. The guide is illustrated with historical public-domain engravings, including etchings by Caspar and Jan Luyken from a 1712 Dutch picture Bible and a woodcut by Julius Schnorr von Carolsfeld (1860).

An introduction covers who Enoch was, the five books inside 1 Enoch, the Dead Sea Scrolls fragments, the book’s place in the Ethiopian Orthodox Bible, and the quotation in the Letter of Jude. Three full-page charts sit beside the days they explain: the gates of the sun in chapter 72, the animals in the Dream Visions, and the ten weeks of history.
