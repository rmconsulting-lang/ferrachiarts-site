Biblical

Six portrait paintings of scripture: Enoch walking a mountain path with a figure in white, the Watchers above a dark valley, Noah’s ark under a rainbow after the flood, Enoch taken up into the light, Jacob asleep beneath a ladder of angels, and a shepherd with his flock at sunset. They are made to hang in a hallway, a reading corner, or above a desk.

Timeless Landscapes

Four quiet landscapes in the same glowing light: a cottage on a misty river at sunrise, a path beside a winding river, a lakeside cottage at sunset with a rowboat in the foreground, and morning sunbeams on a forest stream. Each one is a portrait file, suited to a living room or a bedroom wall.

American West

Six wide paintings of western light: a cowboy looking across red buttes, cattle crossing a river, Rocky Mountain peaks at golden hour, a homestead with a wagon and windmill, bison on the prairie at sunrise, and a bull elk with cows in an autumn meadow. The files are horizontal, for a den, a mantel, or a long wall.
